% Text for the Profiling Overhead subsection:
Nsight Systems costs M15 at most 2.6\,\% of its throughput in any stage or
power mode (Table~\ref{tab:profiler-overhead}), because its frame time is
dominated by GPU kernels. YOLOv8n loses 4--10\,\% in the early stages and 23\,\%
in the FP16 stage at 25~W, the stage that issues the most CUDA API calls per
millisecond. At 15~W the same stage loses only 4\,\%: with the GPU clock
limited to 612~MHz, the pipeline waits for the GPU, and the profiler's
per-call CPU cost is hidden behind it. The overhead of tracing therefore
depends on whether the pipeline is CPU- or GPU-bound, not on the model alone.
Under the profiler, six of the fifteen M15 runs at 25~W registered one to four
over-current events, without a measurable effect on throughput; no run
without the profiler did.
